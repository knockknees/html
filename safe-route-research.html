What the terrain told us

Before Safe Route routes a single road, it was grounded in field interviews and desk research across the hill corridors of Assam, Meghalaya and Sikkim — where landslides, monsoon washouts and patchy connectivity shape how people actually travel.

The field survey

Our primary dataset: structured interviews with the people who actually drive these routes, run Sept 2025 through Feb 2026.

Assam
Lower Assam hill-approach roads feeding into Meghalaya — frequent monsoon washouts and long detours cited most often.

Meghalaya
Shillong plateau routes — landslide-prone stretches where drivers report avoiding certain roads entirely after heavy rain.

Sikkim
Gangtok-area mountain roads — steep gradients and narrow single-lane sections drove demand for multicriteria, not just shortest-path, routing.
